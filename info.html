Returns and exchanges

If you receive a product with damaged packaging or the wrong product, write to us within 7 days of receipt. We will replace the product or refund your money.

You can also withdraw from the purchase within 14 days of receipt without giving a reason, in line with EU consumer law (Directive 2011/83/EU). For health and hygiene reasons, this does not apply to sealed cosmetic products that have been opened after delivery.

FAQ

Are the products suitable for coloured hair?

Yes. Many KACH products protect colour, for example Shampoo RH, Conditioner RH and Conditioner RR. For blonde hair there is Only 1 One Violet.

Do they contain sulphates or parabens?

No. KACH products are made without parabens, sulphates and formaldehyde.

Which shampoo should I use before a treatment?

For deep cleansing before treatments, use Shampoo AC (acidic) or Shampoo DC (alkaline). If you are not sure, write to us and we will help.

How do I pay for my order?

By card online right when you place the order. After payment, our manager will contact you to arrange delivery.
